# Worklog - 20 November 2026: Merchant Center verification tag (1.7.6)

## Summary

The owner started Google Merchant Center's "verify that you manage vexatoys.com" step and received an HTML tag. Release 1.7.6 adds it as `verification.google` in the root metadata, which Next.js renders as `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="...">` in the head of every page, both locales.

## Why

Merchant Center wants the tag in the home page head before the "Verify" click. Putting it in the shared metadata object rather than a raw tag keeps it next to the robots and Open Graph settings and covers `/` and `/ar` from one place.

## How

- `src/layouts/RootDocument.tsx`: `verification: { google: '…' }` on `baseMetadata`, with a comment saying why it must stay.
- `tests/local-seo-signals.test.mjs`: asserts exactly one verification entry with this code.
- `package.json` 1.7.6, changelog. `CACHE_VERSION` unchanged: no data, schema or cached-state change.

## Where

- `src/layouts/RootDocument.tsx`, `tests/local-seo-signals.test.mjs`, `package.json`, `CHANGELOG.md`, this worklog

## Testing

- Unit Tests: `node --test --experimental-strip-types tests/*.test.mjs`.
- Manual Testing: dev server, `/` and `/ar` head contains the meta tag once.
- End-to-End Tests: after deploy, `curl -s https://vexatoys.com/ | grep google-site-verification` shows the tag; then the owner clicks Verify in Merchant Center.

## Advanced Warnings

- Merchant Center warns that vexatoys.com is already claimed by another Merchant Center account. Continuing past Verify to the claim step removes that account's claim and marks its products "Not approved". The owner should confirm the other account is theirs (an older or agency account) before claiming, or reclaim from inside that account instead.

# Worklog - 20 November 2026 (later): one page for the phrase, second pass (1.7.7)

## Summary

The owner asked for a double check of home vs `/sex-toys`. Live comparison: the phrase "sex toys in Lebanon" appears once on the home page and seven times on `/sex-toys`; every blog article's "Shop what this covers" box rendered "Sex Toys in Lebanon →" linking to `/sex-toys`; the article auto-linker sent the phrase there too. Release 1.7.7 turns those signals toward the home page, and a SQL file rewrites the `/sex-toys` guide.

Also settled today from Google's policy pages: Merchant Center lists Lebanon under "Not allowed" for adult-oriented content, so product listings cannot be approved; and the Business Profile guidelines bar age-restricted products from a service-area profile without a storefront. Both are off the plan unless the owner has a staffed location. Links are the remaining off-site lever (`docs/backlinks-strategy.md`).

## How

- `src/routes/blogArticle.tsx`: the sex-toys entry of "Shop what this covers" links to `/` (localized), label unchanged, so about thirty articles now carry the exact-phrase anchor to the home page.
- `lib/blogContent.ts`: two rules at the top of `CATEGORY_LINKS` link the English and Arabic phrase to `/` under slug `home`; `relatedCategorySlugs` filters that slug so it never becomes a shop card. The bare "sex toys" rule still links the category.
- `docs/sql/2026-11-20-sex-toys-guide-rewrite.sql`: English guide rewritten as the online sex shop page without the phrase, "#1 destination" or "500+ products", md5-guarded; Arabic opening sentence replaced by exact match.
- Test added to `tests/local-seo-signals.test.mjs` (text-based: `lib/blogContent.ts` has extensionless imports and cannot load under `node --test`).
- Package 1.7.7, changelog. `CACHE_VERSION` unchanged.

## Where

- `src/routes/blogArticle.tsx`, `lib/blogContent.ts`, `tests/local-seo-signals.test.mjs`, `package.json`, `CHANGELOG.md`, new `docs/sql/2026-11-20-sex-toys-guide-rewrite.sql`, `docs/Database.md`, this worklog

## Testing

- Unit Tests: `node --test --experimental-strip-types tests/*.test.mjs`.
- Manual Testing: dev server, an article page shows the "Sex Toys in Lebanon →" card with `href="/"`, and an article body with the phrase links it to `/` while a bare "sex toys" mention still links `/sex-toys`; Arabic article the same with `/ar`.
- End-to-End Tests: after deploy and the SQL run, `/sex-toys` text no longer contains the phrase; Search Console Performance, filtered to the phrase, should shift impressions from `/sex-toys` to `/` over the following weeks.
