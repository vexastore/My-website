# Worklog - 18 November 2026

## Summary

Release `1.7.4`: the home page becomes the only page targeting "sex toys in Lebanon", the `/sex-toys` page moves to "Online Sex Shop in Lebanon", internal links with the target anchor point home, and the site-wide schema becomes an `OnlineStore` with the Business Profile phone and service area.

## Why

The owner asked why the site does not appear for "sex toys in lebanon". A live check showed the home page (title "Sex Toys Lebanon", H1 "Sex Toys Lebanon") and `/sex-toys` (meta title "Online Sex Shop in Lebanon", H1 "Sex Toys in Lebanon") both targeting the phrase, which splits the signal. The site also carried no phone number, service area or store schema that Google could match against the Business Profile being set up this week.

## How

- `src/data/categories.ts`: Sex Toys `titlePage`/`seoTab` -> "Online Sex Shop in Lebanon | Vexa Toys" (Arabic "متجر جنسي أونلاين في لبنان | متجر فيكسا"). `splitHeroHeading` renders it as ONLINE SEX SHOP / IN LEBANON. The page's meta title and description come from Supabase and already used the shop phrase.
- `src/components/HeroSection.tsx`: home intro opens with "Vexa Toys sells sex toys in Lebanon online: ..." (Arabic mirrors it) and names Beirut same-day delivery.
- `src/components/About.tsx`: hero button links to `/`; delivery block gains "A Lebanese store serving every governorate. Questions? WhatsApp only: +961 76 730 767". `src/routes/quiz.tsx`: quick link "Sex Toys in Lebanon" -> `/`. `src/components/SiteFooter.tsx`: "Delivery across all Lebanon, cash on delivery" under the brand.
- `src/layouts/RootDocument.tsx`: `Organization` -> `OnlineStore` (same `@id`, so the `WebSite.publisher` link holds) with name "Vexa Toys", `alternateName`, `telephone`, WhatsApp `contactPoint`, `address.addressCountry` LB, `areaServed` Lebanon, `currenciesAccepted`, `paymentAccepted`, `priceRange`. No `sameAs` yet: the owner has not confirmed Instagram, Trustpilot or Business Profile URLs.
- `CACHE_VERSION` `v1.7.4`, package 1.7.4, changelog.
