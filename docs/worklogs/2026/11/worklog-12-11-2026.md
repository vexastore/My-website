# Worklog - 12 November 2026

## Summary

Arabic names and descriptions for the 53 products that had none (delivered as `product-arabic-content.sql`, applied by the owner), and `FAQPage` structured data on every category page built from the same FAQ list the page renders. Release `1.5.19`.

## Why

Arabic visitors saw English product names and descriptions on 53 of 76 product pages. Category pages rendered FAQs but emitted `FAQPage` markup only in English and only when an editorial row existed, so most categories and all Arabic pages had none.

## How

- Three parallel writers translated and localized the English descriptions into Modern Standard Arabic (facts only, no additions); an assembler validated Arabic presence, length, closing sentence and absence of English sentences, and wrote the SQL plus a review copy. Two Arabic SEO titles that transliterated "Grip" as a word meaning "strange" were corrected in the same file.
- `lib/categoryFaq.ts` centralises FAQ selection and schema building; the guide section and both category routes call it.
