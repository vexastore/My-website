# Worklog - 25 November 2026: home page takes the exact phrase (1.8.0)

## Summary

First clean Search Console reading after the 1.7.4 to 1.7.8 series. Query "sex toys in Lebanon", last 3 months: 385 impressions, 41 clicks, average position 9.2. Impressions were near zero until 16 November and then ran at 20 to 30 a day, so the consolidation worked. But the page Google shows is `/about` (174 impressions over 3 months, 98 of the last 99), then `/sex-dolls` and `/sexual-enhancers`; the home page and `/sex-toys` have never appeared. Release 1.8.0 gives the home page the exact phrase in its title, H1, one section heading and a site-wide footer anchor.

## Why

The home title and H1 said "Sex Toys Lebanon", without "in"; the body used the exact phrase once. `/about` had the exact phrase as a call-to-action link, H2s "How Vexa Toys Started in Lebanon" and "Discreet Delivery Across Lebanon", and OnlineStore schema with a Lebanon address, so for an exact-phrase query it read as the better answer. The `/about` link already pointed at the home page, so its relevance flows there; the home page now also says the phrase itself, and every page links it with the phrase as anchor text.

## How

- `src/routes/home.tsx`: `SEO_TITLE` and `HERO_HEADING_EN` with "in"; `src/components/HeroSection.tsx`, `src/components/ProductList.tsx`, `src/utils/heroHeading.ts` defaults and comments follow. `splitHeroHeading` splits on "|", so the H1 renders "SEX TOYS IN LEBANON" / "DISCREET ADULT TOYS".
- `src/components/ShoppingInfoSection.tsx` (rendered only when `isHome`): heading "Buying sex toys in Lebanon: what to know before ordering", Arabic equivalent.
- `src/components/SiteFooter.tsx`: the text link under the logo, already `href` home, reads "Sex toys in Lebanon, delivered discreetly" / Arabic with the Arabic phrase. One exact-phrase anchor to the home page on every page of the site.
- Tests: three files re-pinned to the new strings; new test checks the footer anchor and its target, the `/about` link target, the home-only heading, and that `ShoppingInfoSection` stays inside the `isHome` branch.
