# Worklog - 9 November 2026

## Summary

Audited the live site (vexatoys.com) and the Search Console performance export, then implemented the first batch of fixes: smaller server-rendered product payloads, length-safe product and category metadata, a de-optimized About page, absolute titles on static pages, noindex plus sitemap exclusion for thin categories, two missing 301s, brand/whitespace normalization at the Supabase reader boundary, and scoped cache headers.
