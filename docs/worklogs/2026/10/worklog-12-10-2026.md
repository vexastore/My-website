# Task: Make the Homepage the Primary Broad-Query SEO Landing Page

## Summary

Implemented release 1.5.15 to make the homepage the strongest broad-query landing page for `Sex Toys Lebanon` and `Adult Toys Lebanon`. Gave category routes unique H1s, added persistent `/adult-toys` and descriptive homepage links, replaced history-only category changes with real Next.js route transitions, normalized legacy `Vexa Store` Supabase category overrides at the public reader boundary, and restored the missing `/about` H1 discovered during full sitemap validation.

## Why

The owner explicitly chose the homepage as the preferred result for general Lebanon adult-product searches. The audit showed that category pages had stronger exact-match titles, every catalog route reused the homepage H1, `/adult-toys` lacked a persistent crawlable link, and client-side category navigation left the source page's title and canonical in the document. Those signals made Google's selected landing page unpredictable.

## How

Updated homepage metadata and visible headings with the target broad queries. Refactored the shared hero to receive the current page heading and split it into presentation lines without changing its semantic H1 text. Derived category hero headings from `getCategoryTitle`, and used server-provided headings for the homepage and `/adult-toys`. Changed category navigation to `useRouter().push()` and allowed the target route to remount with its own metadata. Added persistent adult-toys links and a descriptive homepage link. Sanitized published category copy as it is mapped from Supabase, then bumped the centralized cache version. Added source-level regression tests and exercised the built application through HTTP and Chrome.
