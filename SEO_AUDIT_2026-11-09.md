## Issues to fix, ranked by impact

### 1. CRITICAL: every page ships the entire product catalog (236 KB of JSON) in the HTML

Evidence: HTML size is 793 KB on `/`, 698 KB on `/sex-toys`, 411 KB on `/dildos`, 311 KB on a single product page, and 316-360 KB on categories with only 2-3 products. In every case the largest inline script is one `self.__next_f.push` chunk of 236 KB carrying `initialProducts` (78 products, each with full `descriptionEn`, images array, SEO fields).

Cause: `app/page.tsx:171`, `app/[category]/page.tsx:300`, `app/[category]/[slug]/page.tsx:311` and `app/adult-toys/page.tsx:128` all pass `productsWithImages` (the whole catalog) into `ShopContext` as `initialProducts`.

Impact: slow LCP/TBT on mobile, wasted crawl budget, and Google has to parse 300+ KB to find 2 KB of content on thin categories.

Fix: pass only the products needed for that page (the category's products, or the single product plus 4-8 related items). Strip description text and secondary images from the list payload. Let the client fetch the rest lazily for search.

### 2. HIGH: HTML is never cached at the CDN (`Cache-Control: no-store` on every page)

Evidence: every HTML response returns `Cache-Control: no-store, no-cache, must-revalidate, max-age=0` and `X-Vercel-Cache: MISS`. TTFB measured 0.57-0.71 s warm and 1.3-4.9 s cold.

Cause: the catch-all `headers()` rule in `next.config.mjs` applies `no-store` to all HTML. The home page also sets `robots: { nocache: true }` (`app/page.tsx:60`), which is not a valid robots directive.

Fix: remove the no-store rule for public pages and rely on ISR (`revalidate`) with `s-maxage=60, stale-while-revalidate=600`. Keep `no-store` only for `/checkout` and `/api/*`. Remove `nocache`.

### 3. HIGH: product page titles are badly formed on 52 of 78 products

Evidence from the sweep of all product pages:

| Problem | Count | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Title longer than 60 chars | 52 | 190 chars: "Thrusting Heating Dildo Vibrator with Remote Control ... \| Sex Toys Lebanon \| Vexa Toys Lebanon" |
| Inconsistent brand suffix | 24 | "Vexa Store Lebanon" (5), "Vexa Store" (2), "Vexa Toys" (11), "Vexa toys", "Vexa", none |
| Meta description outside 70-165 chars | 55 | 50 products are ~300 chars (truncated at 300 by `description.slice(0, 300)`) |
| Typos / junk in title | ~8 | "Viagra_50mg", "Cialis Tadalafil vexa toys", "Chasity cage", "Remote Contro dildo", "Denial Official Chastity  – Metal" (double space), "(59.00 USD)" in title |

Cause: the product title is built as `<product name> | <category> Lebanon | Vexa Toys Lebanon`, so long product names from the admin produce triple-suffixed titles. Several products have hand-written `seoTitle` fields using the old "Vexa Store" brand.

Fix: in the product metadata builder, use `seoTitle` if set, else `<short product name> in Lebanon | Vexa Toys` capped at 60 chars, and drop the category segment. Cap descriptions at 155 chars from a dedicated `seoDescription` field, not the long product text. Clean the ~15 bad names in the admin.

### 4. HIGH: duplicate products indexed as separate URLs

Evidence: three near-identical pairs are live, in the sitemap, and self-canonical:
- `/male-toys/denial-official-chastity-cage-metal-design` and `/male-toys/denial-official-chastity-metal-design` (same price, same copy)
- `/male-toys/manual-penis-vacuum-pump-compact-hand-pump-prod-zv63i3e7j` and `/male-toys/manual-penis-vacuum-pump-compact-hand-pump`
- `/sex-toys/anal-cleansing-douche-easy-comfortable-cleaning` and `/sex-toys/premium-anal-cleansing-douche-easy-comfortable-cleaning-310-ml`

Also slugs with generated suffixes (`-prod-zv63i3e7j`, `-prod-g7v7fpyt8`) and slugs cut mid-word at 60 chars (`...-air-p`, `...-suct`, `...-adu`, `...-g`).

Fix: delete or merge each duplicate in the admin and add a 301 from the loser to the winner in `next.config.mjs` redirects (the pattern already exists there). Rename the truncated slugs with redirects.

### 5. MEDIUM: thin and mismatched category pages

Evidence (products per category): Lingerie 1 (and the single item is a BDSM restraint set), Sex Machines 2, Kegel Balls 2, New Arrivals 3, Lubricants 3, Poppers 3, Strap-Ons 4. The category H2 repeats the title verbatim with the brand pipe ("Dildos in Lebanon | Vexa Toys") instead of being a real heading.

Impact: Google treats near-empty collections as low quality; "Lingerie in Lebanon" ranks for nothing while showing a restraint set.

Fix: either stock the category (3+ real products and 150+ words of unique intro text) or noindex it and remove it from the sitemap until it has products. Fix the lingerie product assignment. Turn the H2 into a descriptive sentence.

### 6. MEDIUM: Arabic version is invisible to Google

Evidence: locale is chosen only by a cookie (`lib/storeLocale.ts`). Googlebot and any request without the cookie get `lang="en"`; there is no `/ar/` URL, no hreflang, no `alternate` link. Arabic keywords sit only in the `keywords` meta tag, which Google ignores. Serving different content on the same URL by cookie also risks a cloaking flag.

Fix: if Arabic search traffic matters, serve Arabic at `/ar/...` (or `ar.vexatoys.com`) with `hreflang="ar-LB"` / `hreflang="en-LB"` pairs and an `x-default`. If not, drop the Arabic meta keywords and leave it as a UI toggle only.

### 7. MEDIUM: static page titles and descriptions outside limits

| Page | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `/privacy` | 82 chars, brand twice ("...Lebanon \| Vexa Toys Lebanon") | 167 chars |
| `/delivery` | 81 chars, brand twice | 189 chars |
| `/warranty` | 75 chars, brand twice | ok |
| `/quiz` | 61 chars, "Vexa Toys Lebanon \| Vexa Toys Lebanon" | ok |
| `/about` | 63 chars, brand twice | 99 chars (short) |
| `/adult-toys` | ok | 98 chars (short) |
| `/dildos` | 26 chars (short, could carry intent) | ok |
| `/sex-toys` | "Sex Toys in  Lebanon" (double space) | ok |
| 15 categories | brand written "Vexa toys" (lowercase t) | - |

Cause: pages set `title: 'X | Vexa Toys Lebanon'` while the root template in `app/layout.tsx` appends `| Vexa Toys Lebanon` again. Category titles come from the `seoTitleEn` field in the database.

Fix: use `title: { absolute: ... }` on those pages (as `/quiz` already does for Arabic), or remove the brand from the page-level title. Fix the DB values for the double space and lowercase brand.

### 8. MEDIUM: blog posts have no images

Evidence: every blog post has exactly 1 `<img>` (the logo), and `og:image` falls back to the generic `opengraph.jpg` (`app/blog/[blogCategory]/[slug]/page.tsx:28`). Article schema has no `image`.

Impact: no Discover eligibility, poor social previews, weaker Article rich results.

Fix: add a 1200x675 featured image per post, set it as `og:image` and `Article.image`.

### 9. LOW: schema details

- Home `robots` meta is `index, follow, nocache`: remove `nocache`.
- `og:locale` is missing on about, blog, quiz and the policy pages (present on categories).
- Product pages show a compare-at price ($97 struck through to $79) but the Offer has no `priceSpecification`/`StrikethroughPrice`; fine for Google, but add it if you want the sale badge in Merchant listings.
- Only 2 products have `aggregateRating`. Collect and display reviews on the top 20 products.
- FAQPage schema on home and categories no longer produces rich results for commercial sites; harmless, but do not spend time on it.
- Content Security Policy header is missing (security, not ranking).

### 10. LOW / policy: prescription medicines listed as products

`/sexual-enhancers/viagra50mg` and `/sex-toys/cialis-5mg` are indexed product pages for prescription drugs. This is a Google Merchant Center / Ads policy violation (healthcare and medicines) and a YMYL quality signal against the whole domain. Decide whether these belong on the site; if kept, noindex them at minimum.
